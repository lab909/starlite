summary: The open-source projects Starlite is built on.

Starlite is glue around proven libraries: [Symfony](https://symfony.com) components,
[Twig](https://twig.symfony.com), [league/commonmark](https://commonmark.thephpleague.com),
[Datastar](https://data-star.dev), [Vite](https://vite.dev), [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com),
[Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) and [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons.
